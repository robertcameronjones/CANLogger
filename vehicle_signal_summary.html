ISO-TP note: VIN (Mode 09 PID 02) is always a multi-frame response requiring ISO-TP reassembly. Flow Control must be sent from the tester to the ECU's physical address. For 11-bit: ECU responds on 0x7E8, FC goes to 0x7E0 (−8). For 29-bit: ECU responds on 0x18DAF115, FC goes to 0x18DA15F1 (swap last two address bytes).

Toyota Hybrid fuel: PID 2F (Fuel Tank Level Input) is not supported on Camry Hybrid. Use UDS DID 1F46 (fuel level in L) or 1022 (alternate) from ECU 0x708 (HV Battery controller). Request must be physical (to 0x700, not broadcast).

Stellantis note: Some Stellantis vehicles (Ram, Dodge, Jeep) use a proprietary CAN network in addition to OBD-II. Witech / CAN-C bus may require specific physical addresses rather than functional broadcast.
